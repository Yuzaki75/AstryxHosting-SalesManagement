# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for contributors, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Focus criticism on the code, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never post customer data, credentials, tokens, or production logs in public threads.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or temporarily ban contributors who violate these rules. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security advisory form:

https://github.com/Yuzaki75/AstryxHosting-SalesManagement/security/advisories/new

All reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly.
